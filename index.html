RuckTrack

A rucking activity app for Garmin watches.

RuckTrack records rucking activities with load-aware calorie estimates using the Pandolf equation. Distance, pace, elevation, heart rate zones, lap data, and pack-bonus calories are tracked on-device and written to a standard Garmin FIT file.
